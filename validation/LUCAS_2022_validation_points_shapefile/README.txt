ZAFM validation points derived from LUCAS 2022

This shapefile contains the 475 LUCAS 2022 points used for technical validation of the ZAFM-WCM and ZAFM-DW datasets.
CRS: EPSG:4326 (WGS 84).

WCM_MATCH and DW_MATCH were recalculated directly from CATEGORY == WCM_GRP and CATEGORY == DW_GRP, respectively.
Audited overall agreement: WCM 268/475 (56.4%); DW 293/475 (61.7%).

Source of LUCAS reference data: European Commission, Eurostat, LUCAS 2022.
https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/lucas/database/2022
This is a derived validation subset and is not an official Eurostat product.
LUCAS photographs are not redistributed here.
